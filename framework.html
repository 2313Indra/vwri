The VWRI Framework

Five Realms of Resilience and 34 Indicators

Framework Structure

VWRI is organised into five Realms of resilience, each assigned a fixed weight reflecting its relative contribution to overall village water resilience. Together the Realms draw on 34 indicators.

Realm Weight Indicators Scope / Key Focus
Hydrological 0.30 8 indicators Groundwater condition, recharge, source density & availability
Infrastructure 0.20 9 indicators Coverage, storage, reliability & pipeline network condition
Water Quality 0.15 4 indicators Chemical & biological safety of drinking water and seasonal stability
Climate 0.15 7 indicators Rainfall variability, drought exposure, warming trend & emissions
Socio-economic 0.20 6 indicators Household income, livestock dependency, migration & governance
Total 1.00 34 indicators Comprehensive Village-Level Water Resilience Index

The Five Realms

Hydrological Resilience Weight 0.30  |  8 indicators

Groundwater condition, recharge, source density and availability of water at the village level.

Infrastructure Resilience Weight 0.20  |  9 indicators

Coverage, storage, reliability and physical condition of water-supply infrastructure.

Water Quality Resilience Weight 0.15  |  4 indicators

Chemical and biological safety of drinking water and its stability across seasons.

Climate Resilience Weight 0.15  |  7 indicators

Rainfall variability, drought exposure, warming trend and mitigation of water-related emissions.

Socio-economic Resilience Weight 0.20  |  6 indicators

Household income, dependency on shared water resources, migration and community governance.

Composite Score Calculation

The final composite index combines all five Realms using their respective weights:

$$\text{VWRI} = 0.30 \times \text{Hydrological} + 0.20 \times \text{Infrastructure} + 0.15 \times \text{Water Quality} + 0.15 \times \text{Climate} + 0.20 \times \text{Socio-economic}$$

(Realm weights sum to 1.00:  $0.30 + 0.20 + 0.15 + 0.15 + 0.20 = 1.00$)
