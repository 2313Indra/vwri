Benefits & Applications

Value of the VWRI Framework for Villages, Implementing Agencies, and Policymakers

Key Framework Benefits

The VWRI framework offers the following key benefits to villages, implementing agencies, and policymakers:

Holistic Assessment

Brings together hydrological, infrastructural, water-quality, climatic and socio-economic dimensions into one comparable score, instead of relying on a single metric.

Evidence-Based Prioritisation

Realm-wise scores pinpoint exactly where a village is weakest (e.g., strong infrastructure but poor groundwater recharge), enabling targeted rather than blanket interventions.

Standardised Benchmarking

Fixed, standards-anchored rating scales (CGWB, BIS 10500:2012, JJM, Falkenmark Index, IMD/WMO, IPCC, NSSO/Census) keep scores comparable across villages and survey rounds.

Monitoring Over Time

Repeat assessment of the same village allows progress (or deterioration) to be tracked against a consistent baseline.

Supports Planning and Funding Decisions

Gram Panchayats, VWSCs/Pani Samitis and line departments can use the composite and Realm scores to plan works and justify budget allocation.

Alignment with National Programmes

Designed to complement Jal Jeevan Mission ‘Har Ghar Jal’ monitoring and SDG 6 (Clean Water and Sanitation) reporting.

Community Ownership

Several indicators (governance, participation, conservation) are drawn directly from VWSC/Pani Samiti records, encouraging local engagement in the assessment itself.

Scalable and Field-Implementable

Built from indicators that can be measured using existing government datasets (CGWB, IMD, JJM-IMIS) supplemented by low-cost household and VWSC surveys.
